# College Chances Calculator India

A free tool for Indian students to see where they stand for college admissions.

Live site: https://pathakshreeja407-alt.github.io/college-chances-/

## What it does
- Choose a stream: IPMAT/BBA, JEE, NEET, CLAT, NID/UCEED or CUET.
- Enter your marks and exam status.
- See each college as Reach, Target or Likely.
- Read the eligibility rules, with links to the official sites.
- Recent cutoffs are shown for JEE and NEET.

## Important
- These are estimates only, not an official admissions prediction.
- Cutoffs and rules change every year. Always confirm on the official website before applying.
- Your marks and answers are not stored or shared with any college.
